## Buyer Card — required fields

The card must contain all of:

```
Name · Photo/Logo · Role · Organization · Country · Verification ·
Collecting Interests · Purpose · Optional Budget · Previous Artbank Activity
```

## Notes

- This is the artist-facing profile of a buyer — distinct from [20-feature-buyer-intent-card.md](20-feature-buyer-intent-card.md), which is the *form* a buyer fills in to express intent on a specific artwork. The Buyer Card is closer to a persistent identity record; the Buyer Intent Card is a per-enquiry submission. Confirm with the team whether these should share a data model.
