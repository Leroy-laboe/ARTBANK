## ⚠️ Hard rule

> **Never reveal anonymous viewers.**

Anonymous traffic (raw view counts) and identified viewers (people who consented to share identity) must be kept visibly and functionally separate — this is one of the items in the [00-overview-and-timeline.md](00-overview-and-timeline.md) completion test ("Interest — anonymous and identified viewers are separated").
